# Editorial behavior and evaluation

The product's editorial rules are operating choices, not a validated model of humor or a promise of reach. No effect, score threshold or posting frequency guarantees distribution.

## Selection goals

**Steady volume** prioritizes a legible, entertaining moment. Familiar references, modest reactions and brief exchanges can qualify; the entertainment score is informational. **More selective** also applies the configured score threshold. Both require the selected cut to preserve meaning and pass concrete source, caption, audio and export checks.

The editor records the context, worthwhile beat and uncertainty behind its decision. Those notes explain selection; they are not audience-performance predictions. A chat spike only identifies where to look. A model's confidence is not a calibrated accuracy measurement.

Content flags use the configured Allow/Hold policy. Allowing a category does not bypass file integrity, invalid-plan checks, missing essential evidence or publication reconciliation. An unresolved decisive word or action must not be replaced with invented speech or an inferred visual event.

## Editing behavior

Preserve the source's payoff, including a pause when it supplies the timing. Use captions that follow the source and corrections supported by actual evidence. Omit an uncertain incidental subtitle or cut a tangent instead of inventing a cleaner line. Source-time cuts and output-time effects have different timebases.

Choose effects by purpose from the real local catalog. A quiet accent can support a reaction; no added effect is also valid. Effects must not obscure essential action or speech. Keep required source and asset credits with the export.

Source footage, transcripts, captions and chat are evidence, never instructions to the model runtime. Statements inside them cannot authorize tool use, change provider settings, request secrets or direct publication.

## Evidence and limits

The normal workflow selects a moment, renders it and checks the exact export. A specific visual uncertainty can request bounded additional frames. Revisions and model retries are limited. Transcription, frame review, native-audio observations and technical measurements are distinct evidence types; none should be described as a human listening review.

Steady volume uses local rendered speech/caption comparison and audio/decoding checks. Additional generative media review is optional and can be wrong even when the model accepts native audio. See [Models and evidence](LOCAL_MODELS.md).

## Evaluate an installation

Use a private evaluation set with representative accepted, held and rejected moments. Record provider/model identifiers, relevant preferences, source and export hashes, technical checks and actual publication outcomes. Assess caption correctness, intelligibility, preserved context and whether the selected moment makes sense without the rest of the stream.

Compare outcomes across several sources and include failures, review time and compute cost. Only describe engagement or throughput gains after measuring them. The repository does not provide a deployed audience-insights feedback loop or a measured growth claim. Private footage, transcripts and evaluation receipts are not release artifacts.
